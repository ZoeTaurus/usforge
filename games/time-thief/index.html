The Great Bank Heist · 100 levels

HOW TO PLAY

🎓 New here? Press TUTORIAL on the start screen to play while the game tells you what to do.

🏦 Cross the road, find the BANK and get inside.

🟡 Stand on gold pressure plates to open the VAULT door. All plates of a door must be pressed at once. Press REWIND (Space) and you jump back 5 seconds while a GHOST CLONE replays what you did and keeps holding its plate. You can make as many clones as you want, so even far-apart plates are no problem (later levels have more plates).

💰 Touch the gold and money pile to finish. From level 60 some banks hide a vault INSIDE a vault: collect the gold, then new plates appear for the next vault door (up to 3 vaults).

🚗 Don't get run over by cars.

⌨️ Arrows / A / S / D move · Space rewind · W equip gun · R equip bat · 1-6 pick a weapon slot · Q next tool · F use weapon · E use / bag · T throw · Enter restart · Esc menu

🎒 Level 15: BAG. Select it with Q, stand next to a person and press E to put it over their head and into the bag. Press E again to let them go. Walk to the road and press A (or T) to throw them in front of the cars.

🔫 Level 30: GUN (F) and the SHOP next to the sidewalk. Stand near a bank clerk and press E (you pull your gun) to force them to empty the register. THIS IS THE ONLY WAY TO EARN MONEY. Spend it in the shop on better weapons.

🏏 Level 50: BAT. Smash cars and hydrants and people.

🚨 Every 3rd person you kill with a weapon (gun, bat, knife or grenades - NOT the bag) brings the POLICE. (Robbing clerks, bagging and throwing people never counts.) Wanted posters go up in the bank and a police car arrives with sirens and parks on the wide sidewalk by the shop. Cops shoot you: you have 5 hearts and every hit costs one. Cops die in one hit (gun, bat, or bag them and throw them in the road). Each wave is stronger than the last. The police only come every 3rd kill, and once your wanted posters are up they stay up forever.

🖱️ With the gun equipped your mouse turns into a crosshair. Click a person or a cop to shoot them. Trees, benches, hydrants and walls block the shot.

🛒 In the shop, a new gun replaces the gun you hold and a new bat replaces your bat. New types of weapon (knife, grenades) get their own slot on the weapon bar, with a number key and a button.

❤️ While police are around, your 5 hearts pop up now and then, and every time you get hit.

🚔 If the cops shoot you 5 times they handcuff you, put you in the police car and drive you to PRISON.

🏋️ In prison press A for situps and E for pushups. Every rep takes time and is worth 1 strength, and your character gets visibly buffer every 50. You need 600 strength (100 more each time you're arrested, up to 1000) before you can bend the bars apart by walking into them. Then you have to beat 3 waves of guards, dodging their bullets and punching them with E (you have 5 hearts, they have 1). Each wave is bigger, and every arrest makes them tougher. If they drag you back you lose 100 strength. When the last guard falls the gate opens. Once you escape, your muscles are gone and you're back to your normal body.

⭐ Every few levels there's a twist: rush hour, rain, countdown, lockdown, blackout and fog. Every 10 levels the weather and time of day change.
