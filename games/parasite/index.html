What am I?

You are small. Smaller than a thumbnail. You have no legs, no eyes, no voice — only hunger, and the knack of slipping inside something that has them.

Burrow into a beetle and you can climb walls. A crow, and you can fly. A person, and you can open doors. But every host leaks into you: their fears, their memories, their instincts.

Stay too long and you'll forget which thoughts were yours.

The world wants you exterminated. Survive it — and stay you.
