    Keeping a colony alive

    Every ant eats. Carry food home faster than the colony uses it, or the stores run dry and sisters starve.

    Inside the nest the queen turns food into eggs. Dig chambers to store more food, raise brood faster and get through winter.

    Each season lasts one day. Autumn is for stockpiling; winter is cold, slow and hungry.

    Staying alive

    Big hunters pick on ants that wander alone. Rally sisters before a fight, and watch for a red ring on the ground: a robin is about to land there.

    If you fall, you wake as another sister. The leaderboard counts how long one ant survives.
