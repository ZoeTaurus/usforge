Unofficial fan-made interactive adaptation
AGAINST THE ODDS
Historical Survival Anthology

Inspired by the survival podcast Against the Odds from Wondery, available through Audible.

This unofficial prototype is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wondery or Audible. Historical events are reconstructed from public records rather than podcast scripts.

Historical simulation rule

History proceeds normally until you interfere.

Major documented events happen on their historical clock. The player can change only their own route, timing and decisions.
