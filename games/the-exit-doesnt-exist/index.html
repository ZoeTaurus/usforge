HOW TO PLAY

W A S D move   Shift run

Mouse look (click the screen to capture it)   Arrow keys also turn and look

E interact with objects   Tab open your notebook   Esc pause

Walk through doors to travel. After the elevator, something wanders the halls. It only moves when you are NOT looking at it. Keep it in view, back away, and slip through a door. If it reaches you, you wake up somewhere else. Watch the clocks and corridor lengths.

Your notebook records anomalies automatically. Progress saves on every door.
