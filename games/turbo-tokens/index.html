  • 🪙 Finish races to earn tokens — 1st place pays the most!
  • ⚡ Earn bonus tokens for overtakes, drifting, top speed, a perfect start, laps in the lead, the fastest lap and a clean race.
  • 🚦 Perfect start: press the gas right when the lights turn green for a free boost.
  • 💨 Drift: hold E while turning. Keep drifting to raise the combo (×2, ×3…). Hitting a wall loses it!
  • 🎬 After each race you see the highlights of your best moments.
  • 🔧 Build your car: buy engines, turbos, tyres, exhausts and nitro with your tokens. Every part shows on your car.
  • 🔥 Drifting refills your nitro tank.
  • 🎚️ Choose Easy, Medium or Hard on the track screen. On Hard the rivals copy your parts and get extra power and grip on top — but it pays ×1.8 tokens.
  • 💀 Insane is way harder: every rival has the best parts in the shop plus extra power and grip. Only the very best drivers win — and it pays ×3 tokens!
  • 🏜️ Harder tracks pay more. The Dune Buggy barely slows down off-road!
  • ❄️ Snowy Peaks is slippery — brake early.
  • 🌙🌧️ Turn on Night or Rain on the track screen for extra tokens. Rain makes the road slippery!
  • 👻 Turn on Ghost to race against your own best lap.
  • 📅 Check the Daily challenges on the menu for bonus tokens every day. In a race, the daily panel shows your progress. Hide it with ✕ or Q, and bring it back with the 📅 button.
  • 🎨 Buy stickers, light trails, horns and bubble colours in the garage's Style tab.
  • 🐠 On Bubble Reef you race in a bubble under the sea — there is no road, just follow the floating stones and coral. Halfway round you shoot out of the water onto a palm island!

📅 DAILY CHALLENGES

Three new challenges every day. Finish a race that does one to win its tokens!
