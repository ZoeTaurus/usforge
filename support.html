Support

Keep the forge burning.

UsForge is free, with no ads and no sign-ups for players. It was started by four friends at Lazy Studios, and it’s now a community of game makers. If you’ve enjoyed a game here, a tip helps us keep making more.

  • Every game on UsForge stays free to play.
  • Tips go to Lazy Studios, the founders: Taurus, Henrique, Alex and Igor.
  • Part of every tip goes into a UsForge fund for site costs and prizes for our community game makers.
  • 1.5% of every tip helps remove CO₂ from the air, through Stripe Climate.
  • Any amount you like. You choose it on the next page.

Payments are handled securely by Stripe, so we never see your card details. If you’re under 18, please ask a parent first. Tips are a thank-you, not a charity donation, and they aren’t refundable.
