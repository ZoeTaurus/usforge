HOW TO PLAY

MOVE Arrow keys or WASD. In the lab and in space you move in any direction. In towns, cities, NASA and on planets you WALK: LEFT/RIGHT, and jump with UP or W.
RECRUIT Touch a monster, scientist or alien to add it to your army.
EAT Hold SPACE while touching one to eat it instead. You mutate even more!
FIND They hide on rooftops, in alleys and in far corners. Up close they appear, far away they only twinkle. Explore everything!
EXIT Capture every target, then reach the glowing exit on the right.
LIVES You have 3. Lose them all and you restart at your last checkpoint flag. A flag turns from red to green when you run past it.
DANGER Lab: lasers, drones, crushers, spikes, turrets. Towns and cities: cars, soldiers, fire, bombs. Planets: meteors, lava, guards. Space: asteroids, black holes, comets, storms.
TOUCH Drag to move. Use the JUMP and EAT buttons. Esc pauses.
GOAL Mutate, grow your army and conquer every town, city, planet, galaxy and universe, one by one.
