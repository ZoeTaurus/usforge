HOW TO PLAY

THE GOAL

Mine ore, carry it back up and sell it at the shop. Buy better gear so you can dig deeper, where ore is worth more. In a career, earn $25,000 to retire. If you die, you lose the run.

BUILD YOUR MINESHAFT

Ladders are how you get back up. Supports hold up the roof for 3 tiles on each side, but only if they reach it: stand them on the floor and stack them until they touch the ceiling (a ❗ means it's too short). Taller rooms need taller columns. Torches light your tunnels. Platforms are planks you can stand on: jump up through them from below and hold S to drop back down. Great for bridges over lava and ledges in big caves. A bridge can only reach 5 tiles from a wall or the ground. For longer spans, stack bridge posts up from the floor under it. Knock out the support and the bridge falls!

Digging straight down is safe. Sideways tunnels without supports will crack and cave in.

DANGERS

Long falls • cave-ins • loose gravel • rising poison gas • flowing lava that burns your ladders. Underground water pours into tunnels: you can swim (hold jump to rise) but watch your breath bubbles. Water hardens lava into obsidian. Your health slowly refills on the surface.

MONSTERS & LOOT

Bats, slimes, cave crawlers and fire wisps lurk in the dark, and get tougher the deeper you go. Click one to hit it with your pick. They drop coins and sometimes gems.

Deeper down: cave spiders drop from the ceiling, lost miners drift through solid rock, and blast beetles explode when they reach you.

Monsters won't spawn near torches. Crack open treasure chests in caves for cash and supplies.

THE DEEP WYRM

At the very bottom of the mine, around 230m, lies a huge chamber. Something lives there. Hit its head for extra damage, dodge the fireballs, and watch the floor when it burrows. Beat it for a fortune in treasure.

GETTING RICH

The shop posts contracts: deliver the ore they want for a big bonus. Watch for HOT ores that sell for +50% for a while.

Save up for spring boots (softer landings) and a jetpack (hold jump in mid-air). Days pass on the surface: at night the stars come out.

TOUCH

Use the arrows to walk, climb and jump. Hold on a block to mine it. Turn on BUILD and tap to place the selected item. Tap the hotbar to pick an item, or tap the medkit / beacon to use them. Hold on a monster to attack it.
