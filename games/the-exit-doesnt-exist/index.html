HOW TO PLAY

W A S D move   Shift run

Mouse look (click the screen to capture it)   Arrow keys also turn and look

E interact with objects   Tab open your notebook   Esc pause

GOAL: find the exit.

Walk through doors to travel. Every floor you reach by elevator has its own different creature (your starting floor is safe). Most only move when you are NOT looking at them (the little one on floor 2 never stops). Keep it in view, back away, and slip through a door. If it reaches you, you wake up somewhere else. Watch the clocks and corridor lengths.

Your notebook records anomalies automatically. Progress saves on every door.
