The infinite store

WHY ARE YOU HERE?

You walked into a huge flat-pack furniture store and never found the way out. By day the faceless employees in yellow shirts ignore you, the arrows on the floor lead nowhere and soft music drifts from somewhere far away. At closing time the lights die, and the staff come looking for you: employees, security, the new hire, the cook, the stocker, the greeter. And the manager.

You can't fight them. Grab furniture, close the doors and wall yourself in before closing time. Keep your flashlight off if you don't want to be seen. Don't look away from the greeter.

WASD walk, SHIFT run
MOUSE aim your light
E / RMB grab, open doors
Q / LMB place furniture
F flashlight, C eat
1-9 / wheel pick furniture
